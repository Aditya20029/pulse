## Project title

Pulse: Global News Intelligence Globe

## One-line tagline

A real-time 3D globe that maps world news as it breaks, with Claude-powered intelligence briefings on any event.

## Short description (for the card / preview)

Pulse is an interactive 3D Earth that visualizes 1,000+ live news events in real time and generates AI intelligence briefings on demand. It aggregates GDELT, 45+ global RSS feeds, Reddit, and Hacker News, geolocates and clusters every story, and renders them on a photoreal 8K globe. Click any hotspot for a streaming analyst briefing powered by Claude Opus 4.7.

## Full description

Pulse turns the firehose of global news into a single, glanceable picture of the world.

It continuously pulls events from GDELT, 45+ international RSS feeds (Reuters, BBC, AP, Al Jazeera, France 24, NHK, South China Morning Post, Globo, and more), Reddit, and Hacker News. Every story is categorized, geolocated (by place mentions or source newsroom), and merged into spatial clusters that pulse on a photorealistic 3D Earth with day and night lighting, city lights, aurorae, live earthquakes, and orbiting satellites.

Click any cluster and Claude Opus 4.7 streams a structured analyst briefing: what happened, why it matters, connected events, historical parallels, key actors, a severity score, and a 12-hour sentiment forecast. You can reframe any briefing through a devil's advocate or counterfactual lens, or translate it into seven languages. A built-in "Ask Claude" assistant answers questions using the live globe state as context, and AI panels surface anomalies, cross-cluster narratives, and 24-hour forecasts.

The mission-control HUD adds a 24-hour time-scrub replay, category layers, regional breakdowns, trending entities, a global sentiment timeline, world clocks, markets and commodities tickers, and embedded live news TV. It is fully responsive with a dedicated mobile and tablet layout, installable as a PWA, and exportable as screenshots, 4K posters, timelapse video, Markdown, and a printable PDF report.
