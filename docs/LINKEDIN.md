## Post (primary version)

I built Pulse: a real-time 3D globe that maps the world's news as it breaks.

It pulls 1,000+ live events from GDELT, 45+ global newsrooms (Reuters, BBC, AP, Al Jazeera, France 24, NHK, South China Morning Post, and more), Reddit, and Hacker News, geolocates every story, and renders them as glowing clusters on a photorealistic Earth.

Click any hotspot and Claude Opus 4.7 streams a full intelligence briefing in about a second: what happened, why it matters, connected events, historical parallels, key actors, and a 12-hour sentiment forecast. You can reframe it through a devil's advocate lens or read it in 7 languages, and an "Ask Claude" assistant answers questions about anything on the globe.

It also has embedded live news TV, a 24-hour time-scrub replay, markets and earthquake overlays, AI anomaly detection and forecasting, and a full mobile layout.

Built solo, end to end:
- Three.js + React Three Fiber with custom GLSL shaders (8K Earth, day/night terminator, aurorae, satellites)
- Next.js 16 + TypeScript, streaming Claude integration with caching and rate limiting
- A real-time pipeline that aggregates, geocodes, and clusters thousands of headlines
- Hardened and deployed on Vercel with a full security pass (CSP, same-origin AI guards, spend caps)

This pulled together everything I love: real-time data, 3D graphics, AI, and product polish.

Live demo and code in the comments. Always open to talking about frontend, full-stack, 3D, or AI engineering roles.

## On tagging news outlets

You asked about tagging big outlets (Reuters, BBC, etc.). Honest take: @mentioning
large brand accounts you are not affiliated with rarely drives reach and can read
as spammy, and LinkedIn may suppress it. They are already named naturally in the
post, which is enough for keyword discovery. If you still want to tag, tag PEOPLE
who will actually engage: classmates, a professor, devs in the Three.js / Next.js
communities, and anyone at companies you are targeting. Real early engagement from
your network is what the algorithm rewards in the first hour, far more than brand tags.

## Posting tips for max reach

- Post Tuesday to Thursday, mid-morning your timezone.
- Put the live link in the first comment, not the body.
- Lead with the one-line hook (done above); the first 2 lines are what people see before "see more".
- Reply to every comment in the first hour to boost the post.
- Attach the demo GIF or a sharp screenshot.
